Focus Areas and Expert Biographies

What We Do

Economic Empowerment and Livelihood Development

BSDA is dedicated to enhancing the economic well-being of communities through vocational training, entrepreneurial support, and assistance in creating sustainable livelihoods. This program is specifically designed for women, youth, and marginalized communities, ensuring they acquire the skills and resources necessary for financial independence.

Key Initiatives:

  • Microfinance and small business loans for women and vulnerable groups.
  • Vocational training centres offering courses in trades such as tailoring, carpentry, and agriculture.
  • Entrepreneurial mentorship and support for youth startups.

Expert: Dr. Ahmed Daahir, PhD in Development Economics.
Dr. Ahmed Daahir is an esteemed development economist with more than 20 years of experience in economic empowerment programs, specializing in rural and urban poverty alleviation. He has collaborated with numerous international organizations, including the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) and the World Bank. Dr. Ahmed has been at the forefront of community-driven microfinance initiatives that have empowered thousands of women throughout Africa. His innovative approaches to economic development underscore participatory models, enabling communities to take the lead in shaping their economic futures. He holds a PhD in Development Economics from the University of Sussex and has published extensively on inclusive growth strategies.

What We Do

Education and Capacity Building

BSDA is committed to enhancing access to quality education, particularly for children and young adults in marginalized communities. Our initiatives emphasize both formal education and informal training programs that strengthen capacity-building for community leaders and educators.

Key Initiatives:

  • Construction and renovation of schools in remote areas.
  • Teacher training programs focused on modern pedagogy and inclusive education.
  • Scholarship programs for underprivileged students.
  • Digital literacy programs to equip students with 21st-century skills.

He has collaborated with the Ministry of Education in various countries to enhance curricula, teacher training, and student performance, particularly in disadvantaged communities. Dr. Adan is dedicated to inclusive education and is recognized for his efforts in implementing innovative educational programs that address gender disparities. He holds a Doctorate in Education (Curriculum Development) and has served as a consultant for the European Union, UNICEF, and UNESCO.

He has led several initiatives aimed at introducing digital tools in rural schools, facilitating students' access to online resources and contemporary learning platforms. Dr. Jama’s projects include the development of digital literacy programs designed for both students and educators, ensuring that education in underserved communities keeps pace with advancements in the digital age. He holds a PhD in Educational Technology and has collaborated with organizations such as the World Bank and the African Union on digital education reforms.

What We Do

Health and Well-being

BSDA aims to improve communities' overall health and well-being by providing access to essential healthcare services, raising awareness about health and hygiene, and addressing pressing health issues like maternal care and infectious diseases.

Key Initiatives:

  • Mobile health clinics for rural and hard-to-reach areas.
  • Awareness campaigns on maternal and child health, HIV/AIDS, and malaria prevention.
  • Sanitation and hygiene programs promoting clean water and safe practices.
  • Collaborations with local health authorities to strengthen community healthcare systems.

Expert: Dr. Hawa Dakir, Public Health Specialist.
Dr. Hawa is an expert in global health with a focus on public health interventions in low resource settings. She has led numerous health programs across Africa, including maternal and child health projects, nutrition improvement initiatives, and infectious disease control efforts. Dr. Hawa is recognized for her work in promoting healthcare access in rural communities, where she has trained local health workers and established mobile health clinics. She holds an MD from the University of Nairobi and a Master’s in Public Health from Johns Hopkins University, where she has served as a visiting lecturer on community health systems.

Environmental Sustainability

BSDA is committed to promoting environmental conservation and sustainable practices that protect natural resources and enhance resilience to climate change. Our programs focus on educating communities about environmental stewardship while implementing practical, community-driven solutions.

Key Initiatives:

  • Reforestation projects to combat deforestation and promote biodiversity.
  • Water conservation and management programs, including rainwater harvesting and sustainable irrigation.
  • Renewable energy initiatives, such as solar energy installations for rural communities.
  • Climate change awareness campaigns to inform communities about environmental risks and sustainable practices.

Dr. Yasmin holds a PhD in Environmental Science from the University of Cape Town and has served as a consultant for the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP).

Community Development and Social Welfare

BSDA’s community development programs focus on improving local governance, promoting social welfare, and encouraging civic participation. We aim to empower communities to take charge of their development by improving infrastructure, providing social services, and enhancing local leadership.

Key Initiatives:

  • Governance training for local leaders and community organizations.
  • Infrastructure development, including the construction of community centers and public amenities.
  • Social services programs that provide support for families affected by poverty, disabilities, or emergencies.
  • Disaster relief programs for communities impacted by natural or man-made crises.

What We Do

Research and Development

BSDA recognizes the vital role of research in creating effective policies and interventions for sustainable community development. Our Research and Development (R&D) division is dedicated to conducting high-quality, evidence-based research that informs our programs and initiatives. By engaging in rigorous data collection and analysis and collaborating with academic institutions, we aim to develop innovative solutions to the challenges facing communities in areas such as education, health, economic empowerment, and environmental sustainability.

Key Initiatives:

  • Conducting baseline and impact assessments for community development projects.
  • Collaborating with universities and research institutes to publish findings that inform local and national policy.
  • Innovating new technologies and approaches for development, especially in education, healthcare, and environmental sustainability.
  • Developing community-driven data collection mechanisms to ensure localized, relevant insights are captured.
